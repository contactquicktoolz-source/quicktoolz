About QuickToolz

Simple tools, built for everyone.

QuickToolz is a collection of free, fast, and easy-to-use online utilities — built to help you get everyday tasks done without any sign-up, subscription, or hidden fees.

Whether you need to compress an image, convert files to PDF, generate a QR code, or count words in your document, QuickToolz gives you instant results right in your browser.

QuickToolz is proudly developed and maintained by Santa Technologies.
